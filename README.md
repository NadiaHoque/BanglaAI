# Alap AI: Bangla AI access platform (demo)

*Alap AI is a working name.*

This is a clickable demo of a platform that would let people in Bangladesh use leading AI models, such as Claude and ChatGPT, in Bangla. They would pay in Taka through mobile financial services or mobile airtime instead of an international card.

**Status:** concept demo for discussion. It is not a working service.

## The idea

- **One local app** that connects users to leading AI models.
- **Local payments** in BDT, from mobile wallets and mobile airtime balance.
- **Affordable pricing** through pay-as-you-go and small weekly or monthly packs.
- **Bangla first**, with starting points for students, small business owners, entrepreneurs and agricultural workers.

## What the demo shows

| Screen | What you can do |
|---|---|
| Home | See your Taka balance and pick a starting point: study, small business, farming or entrepreneurs. |
| Chat | Choose Claude or ChatGPT and ask a sample question in Bangla. Each message comes off your balance, and when it runs out you are prompted to add money. |
| Wallet | Add money pay-as-you-go (৳20 to ৳200) or buy a weekly or monthly pack, paying by mobile wallet or mobile airtime. |

A language button switches every screen between Bangla and English.

## What is simulated

- AI replies are pre-written samples. No model is called.
- No payment is taken, and the balance resets when the demo reloads.
- Prices (৳0.50 per message, ৳49 a week, ৳149 a month) are placeholders, not a pricing proposal.
- Payment methods are generic. No partnership with any provider is implied.
